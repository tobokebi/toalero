A Letter For You

To my Alero 🩵

To my Alero, 🩵

I honestly don't know where to begin, but I know there's something I've wanted to tell you, and I figured you deserved more than just a random text or a simple question thrown into an ordinary conversation.

I want you to understand that this isn't just about giving us a label or being able to call you my girlfriend. It's about wanting to build something meaningful with you. Something that goes beyond the excitement of the beginning and continues to feel worth choosing even as time passes.

I know we're both human. We have our individual personalities, habits, imperfections, moods, and moments when we might not understand each other. I don't expect you to be perfect, and I don't intend to pretend that I am. I want us to have the freedom to be ourselves while still making conscious efforts to become better for each other.

I want to know you beyond the obvious things. I want to understand the little things that make you happy, the things that bother you, the dreams you sometimes hesitate to talk about, the things you find funny, and even the random thoughts that cross your mind. I want to be someone you can talk to without feeling like you have to filter every part of yourself.

I want us to laugh until we forget what started the laughter, have random conversations that somehow last for hours, create memories out of the most ordinary days, and experience things that we'll look back on someday with the biggest smiles.

But I also want us to understand that a meaningful relationship isn't built on the happy moments alone.

There will be misunderstandings. There may be days when one of us is tired, overwhelmed, or simply not at our best. When those moments come, I don't want us to become strangers to each other. I want us to communicate, listen, understand, apologise when necessary, and remember that we're on the same team.

I don't want either of us to feel like we have to compete for attention, beg for reassurance, or constantly question where we stand. I want consistency, consideration, honesty, respect, and the kind of effort that doesn't disappear once the excitement settles.

And I want to support your dreams just as much as I want you to support mine. I want to see you win, celebrate your progress, encourage you when things don't go as planned, and watch us both become better versions of ourselves. I hope we can inspire each other, learn together, try new things, and achieve things we might not have imagined doing together.

I don't have every detail of the future figured out, and I won't pretend that I do. But I know that if we're both willing, there's so much we can experience, learn, build, and become together.

I want something genuine. Not something that only looks beautiful from the outside, but something that actually feels beautiful to the two people living it.

And if there's one thing I hope you take away from all of this, it's that I don't want to simply have you in my life. I want to make sure you feel appreciated, respected, heard, and genuinely cared for while you're in it.

Here's to the possibility of something beautiful, to new memories, honest conversations, silly moments, shared dreams, and a journey we both get to have a say in.

Whatever your answer, thank you for being someone worth putting all this thought into.

Always yours,
